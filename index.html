About Nexor

Built to Help Restaurants Present, Promote, and Sell Better

Nexor is a restaurant-focused platform built to bring menus, offers, and digital operations into one clear customer experience. Our goal is simple: help restaurants look more professional, reduce ordering friction, and give every customer a smoother dining journey.
